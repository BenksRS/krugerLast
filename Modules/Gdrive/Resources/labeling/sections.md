# Report sections

Every photo is assigned to exactly ONE of the sections below, decided only from
what is visible in that photo. Sections are used to organize the professional
PDF report. The photos stay in chronological (source-folder) order — the section
is just a heading; do not force a photo into the wrong section to keep a block
tidy.

Use the section name **verbatim** (exact spelling and casing).

- **Property Overview** — front of home, driveway, entrance, street view, general
  property/exterior shots that are not focused on damage or work.
- **Damage Before Work** — storm damage as found on arrival: fallen/leaning trees
  on the structure, fence or yard, damaged trunks, tree resting on a screen
  enclosure, roof/structure damage, before any removal work has started.
- **Tarp Installation** — a roof tarp being installed or an already-installed roof
  tarp. Requires visible tarp material (poly/felt sheet) with edges/seams and wood
  battens or sandbags. A bare shingled roof, or a worn/lighter patch of shingles,
  does NOT go here — use Damage Before Work or Completed Work.
- **Tree Removal Operations** — active emergency tree work: crew cutting with a
  chainsaw, sectioning a fallen tree, rigging, lifting or hauling limbs, chipper
  or truck in use.
- **Debris Removal** — loading, hauling or staging storm debris on the property
  (not yet at the curb).
- **Yard Clean** — the yard after the tree and debris are gone: raked, cleared
  ground, restored area.
- **Completed Work** — the finished result: structure/fence/enclosure with the
  hazard removed, final condition shots.
- **Debris Curbside** — debris piled at the street/curb for municipal pickup.
- **Measurements** — tape measure or ruler against a trunk, limb or damaged area
  documenting size/diameter.
- **Other** — anything that does not clearly fit above.

## Canonical order (for the report)

- Property Overview
- Damage Before Work
- Tarp Installation
- Tree Removal Operations
- Debris Removal
- Yard Clean
- Completed Work
- Debris Curbside
- Measurements
- Other
